# 🟩 Pixel-Commit

*A PIXEL7 MEDIA project*

**Design your activity. Plan your progress. Visualize your GitHub journey.**

Pixel-Commit is a free website that lets you draw on a picture of the GitHub activity calendar, the grid of green squares on your profile. You pick a letter, a number, a shape, or your own drawing, and see exactly how it would look. Then you get a simple day-by-day schedule you can follow.

---

## What problem does it solve?

Your GitHub profile shows a calendar of green squares. Most people never think about how it looks, and many find it hard to stay consistent. There is also no easy way to plan ahead or to "see" a goal on that calendar.

Pixel-Commit fixes this by letting you:

- **See** your idea on a real-looking calendar before anything happens.
- **Plan** which days you will work and which days you will rest.
- **Save** your design as a picture or a schedule you can keep.

## What can you do with it?

| Feature | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| ✍️ **Type a word** | Type your name or initials and watch them appear as little squares. |
| 🎨 **Draw by hand** | Click squares on or off to make any shape you like. |
| 🖼️ **Use a picture** | Upload a small image and it turns into a square-by-square design. |
| 📚 **Ready-made designs** | Hearts, stars, waves, stairs and more, just pick one. |
| 🌗 **Light and dark shades** | Choose how strong each square looks, and pick your favorite green. |
| 📅 **Pick your dates** | Choose when your design starts and how many weeks it covers. |
| 📋 **Daily plan** | See a clear list of "work day" and "rest day" for every date. |
| 💾 **Download** | Save as a picture (PNG or SVG), a spreadsheet (CSV), or add reminders to your phone or computer calendar. |

## An honest note

Pixel-Commit is a **planning and design tool**. It does **not** make any changes to your GitHub account, and it does not create fake activity.

A square on GitHub only turns green when you do real work, such as saving a real change to a real project. So treat the plan as a friendly reminder to build something meaningful, one day at a time. The design is the goal, and the real work is the way to get there.

## Who is it for?

- Developers who like their profile to look unique.
- Students who want a simple way to build a daily habit.
- Anyone who enjoys creative, pixel-style art.

## Try it yourself

You need a computer with [Node.js](https://nodejs.org) installed.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/<your-username>/pixel-commit.git
cd pixel-commit
npm install
npm run dev
```

Then open **http://localhost:3000** in your browser. No sign-up and no GitHub login needed.
